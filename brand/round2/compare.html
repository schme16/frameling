Frameling mascot, round two

Five caterpillar-led concepts, each drawn from code in this folder. Every asset is shown at 256, 64, 32 and 16 px on dark and light. The model sheets are shown at 256 and 128 px wide, since a three-pose strip is unreadable below that. On dark the ink outlines sink into the background, which is the honest answer to how the mascot sits on a dark page.
