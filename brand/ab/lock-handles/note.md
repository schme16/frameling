# Locked: the handles just go

A locked rail or set of panels loses the grip, the width edge and the window handles, and nothing is put in their
place. The small lock that used to sit where the grip was is gone from the rail, the tab bars, the right side and
floating windows.

- **rail-before-dark / rail-before-light**: unlocked, with the grip dots at the top.
- **rail-after-dark / rail-after-light**: locked. No grip, no lock, just the tools.
