Chalk and charcoal investigation (board doc: chalk-charcoal, card: chalk-charcoal-look). Branch wip/chalk.

<brush>-strip-{dark,light}.png: one per brush (13: the eleven that lean on the chalk and charcoal tips, plus Chalk and Charcoal
themselves). Each has four rows and four test marks across (firm, light, pressure ramp, three passes over one patch):
  Now             what ships today: one solid fill with holes punched out, grain grid fixed to the page
  Holes only      tip.trial "edge+press+seed": more holes near the edge, more where the pen was light, own holes per line
  Layered         tip.trial "layers+seed": six see-through fills, a wide pale one and narrower darker ones where the pen was pressed
  Layered + grain tip.trial "all": the layered model plus the hole tricks
all-before-{dark,light}.png and all-layers-{dark,light}.png: every brush on one sheet, Now and Layered.

Colour: dark ink on cream paper (light theme), cream ink on near-black (dark theme); Colour pencil, Soft pastel, Oil pastel,
Crayon use a colour so tone and build-up show in a hue.
Made with: npm run build; xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1600x1000x24" npx electron scripts/ab/chalk.cjs <rawDir> <brushes.json> <label>;
then python3 scripts/ab/chalk-sheets.py <rawDir> brand/ab/chalk before=Now cheap=Holes\ only layers=Layered all=Layered\ +\ grain
No preset was changed. The trials are reached only by the optional tip.trial word.
