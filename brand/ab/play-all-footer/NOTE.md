# Play all in order: footer

`ab.png`: the bottom of the Play all in order window, with shots of different sizes. Top row: Picture size on First shot. Bottom row: Picture size on Custom (the widest case). Left column A, right column B.
A (before): everything on one row, which wraps and stacks Fit/Fill once Custom shows its Width and Height. B (built, the default): two labelled rows, "Picture size" and "Other sizes" (Fit or Fill), above a quiet status, Loop and Play row. It costs about 45 px of height in the First shot case. Tab order is unchanged: shots, Picture size, Fit or Fill, Loop, Play.
Another option if height matters: put Picture size and Other sizes in one small "Look" menu button. Not built.
