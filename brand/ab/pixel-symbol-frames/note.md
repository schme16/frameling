# A symbol that holds a whole animation

Pictures in the dark and the light theme (`-dark` and `-light`). Nothing here is an A against a B; they are the two new moments to judge by eye.

- `frames-question-*.png`: the first time you press F8 (Make a symbol) on a layer with several drawings. It asks once, **The whole animation** (the starting choice) or **Just this drawing**, and remembers. Change it in Preferences, Behaviour.
- `pixel-offer-*.png`: the in-between offer after clicking a frame between two pixel drawings. Before, nothing showed on a pixel layer. Only **Move** is offered (pixels have no outlines to morph).

The clip for the help topic is `public/help/symbols-frames-dark.mp4` and `symbols-frames-light.mp4` (H.264, yuv420p).
