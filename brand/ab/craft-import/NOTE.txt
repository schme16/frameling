Importing the two sample files, each in dark and light.

vectorcraft-dusk-poster-{dark,light}.png
  dusk-poster.vectorcraft opened with File > Open (or File > Import > VectorCraft file). A 600 x 800 drawing with one
  layer: 39 shapes and 2 pieces of text (the title stays editable text), the sky, sun and lake gradients, the sun's glow,
  and 46 colours in the swatches. The toast says what was left out.

vectorcraft-left-out-{dark,light}.png
  The same import, after pressing "See what was left out": the sun's glow blend mode, one shape with no paint at all,
  shape names (Frameling shapes have none), and gradient swatches (swatches here are single colours).

photocraft-test-{dark,light}.png
  test.pcraft opened as a drawing of its own: one layer ("Background"), 640 x 360, nothing left out. The pixels are
  exactly the ones in the file's own flattened preview (a test checks this). The unrelated Twitch toast is the harness's
  usual one, it is in every help picture.

Made in xvfb with scripts/docs/help-media.cjs --entries (entries kept outside the repo). The help topics
import-vectorcraft and import-photocraft have their own pictures in public/help (both themes).
