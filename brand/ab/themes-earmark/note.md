# The earmark's ink line

The fold's yellow is cut with a clip whose points are percentages of the badge's **border** box, while the ink line
was drawn in a box the size of the width and height alone. The padding that nudges the sun or moon into the thick
part of the fold made the two boxes different sizes (40 px against 54 px), so the line ran through the middle of the
yellow instead of along its edge.

- **before-day-card / before-dark / before-light**: the black line cuts across the yellow.
- **after-day-card / after-dark / after-light**: one box for both, so the line lies right on the yellow's edge. The
  fold is the same size on screen as before.
