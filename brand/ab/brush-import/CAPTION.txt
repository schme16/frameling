Brush import: a stroke from each real brush file (GIMP .gbr, .gih, .vbr and Photoshop .abr), read by the real importer and drawn by the real pixel brush, in both themes, each beside its name and the settings Frameling kept. Neither program is on this machine, so there is no picture of the same brush in its original program: judge them by what each brush is meant to be. Honestly: the plain round, square and pencil brushes come close; picture tips are shrunk to 32 by 32 pixels, so charcoal and oil tips are blockier than in GIMP; Photoshop brushes that lean on scatter, dual brush and texture come in as the nearest thing Frameling has and look like themselves only in spirit (the card on each tile says how much was left out or changed); the angle direction of a flattened tip is unchecked against the real programs.
