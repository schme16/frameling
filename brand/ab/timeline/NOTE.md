Timeline look (board task 16). Shot at 1400x900 in the Animate workspace, Bouncing logo and Paper doll puppet, dark and light.

- A: Clean timeline, today's look (one plain block per drawing). Kept as a preference: Preferences > General > Clean timeline, View > Clean timeline, or the sliders button in the timeline header.
- B (built as the default): Detailed timeline. Keys are solid cells with a diamond, holds are paler bands ending in a small box, automatic holds are striped, tweens are purple arrows and morphs green arrows with the easing drawn in. Layers open (arrow) into property rows (position, size, turn, see-through, tint, camera view, each bone, each animated effect) with diamonds you can pick (click, Shift+click, box), drag to retime, Alt+drag to stretch, Delete to remove. Ruler shows seconds over frames; markers draw a dashed line through the rows; layer header gets colour labels and a solo (S) switch.

Files: ab-<example>-<theme>.png (A above B, timeline area), <example>-<theme>-a.png / -b.png (whole window), b-retime-keys.mp4 (H.264: pick a key, Shift+click one on another layer, drag both 5 frames later, box-pick keys on the property rows, drag them 3 frames earlier).
