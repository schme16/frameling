# Picking a theme

- **after-dark / after-light**: Basic and Advanced are gone. Every theme is in one grid, and pressing one asks what
  it is for: by day, at night, or both. The one in use wears a sun or a moon in the corner of its preview, a tick by
  its name and a gold edge. The two themes card at the top is unchanged.
- **tidy-dark / tidy-light**: the description is a tooltip now rather than a block of text, Make it mine says
  Duplicate and edit, and the buttons lie over the foot of the card while you point at it, so the card is the same
  size whether they show or not and the grid never shifts.
- **earmark-dark**: the badge is a folded corner now, gold, with the moon on your night theme and the sun on your
  day one, instead of a small round dot. Both means a bigger corner with the two icons on it.
- **earmark-curved-dark / earmark-curved-light**: the fold is curved now, like a page corner turned over, and the
  sun or moon on it is bigger and sits where the fold is widest.
