Selecting a gradient shape: the Colours panel shows its gradient and edits it.

A (before-pick-*): a sky with a gradient fill and a plain outline, picked with the Select tool. The panel shows the
   stroke box and a plain colour picker with Solid lit. The gradient the shape has is nowhere to be seen.
B (after-pick-*): the same pick. The fill comes to the front, Linear is lit, the bar shows the shape's three
   colours, and Direction shows its angle.
Clip (after-edit-*): the middle marker is dragged along the bar, then the last one is picked and recoloured with
   the picker. The sky on the canvas changes as it goes. H.264, 1000 wide, both themes.
