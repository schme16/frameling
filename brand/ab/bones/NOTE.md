# Bone look on the canvas

`bones-ab.png`: the Paper doll puppet with the Pose tool out, frame 3. Left pair dark theme, right pair light (the
stage looks the same in both).

- **A (default): kite.** The classic pointed bone shape, translucent grape with an ink edge, big dot at the joint,
  small white dot at the tip (the IK handle). Reads as "bone" at a glance and shows which way each bone points.
- **B: slim.** Rounded grape lines with the same joint and tip dots. Hides less of the art, busier to tell apart.

Both ship; B is Rig > Slim bones. The picked bone turns sunny gold, pinned joints are rose with a pin head.
