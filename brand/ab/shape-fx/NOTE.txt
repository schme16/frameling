Effects on one shape, not just a whole layer, in dark and light.

A soft shadow under a character (shadow-*) and a glow round a title (glow-*), each done two ways:
  -old    the shape on a layer of its own, with the effect on that layer (the only way until now; the Shadow or Title layer is in the list)
  -new    the shape picked on the one Lines layer, with the effect on the shape (no extra layer)
The pictures on the canvas match: it is the same soften and glow, drawn by the same code.
  -panel  the Effects panel with the shape picked: "Shape: the one you picked" at the top, no blend or clip, and no key button.
