Tooltips: another pass. Same tips, old build (before) against the new one (after), dark and light.

What changed
- Above, not below. The tip sits just above the thing and drops under it only when there is no room up there. A menu row's tip
  still goes beside the whole menu, and a tool at the left edge still gets its tip on its right.
- Its own surface. The tip uses the theme's text colour as its background and the page colour as its words: a cream label in
  the dark theme, an ink one in the light theme, so it never reads as another menu or panel. A custom theme follows its own colours.
- An arrow. A small diamond on the tip's edge nearest the thing, sliding along it to keep pointing at the middle of the thing even
  when the tip was pushed sideways to stay on screen, and left off when the tip can't be put near the thing.
- The tip follows its thing if it moves while the tip is up (the arrow slides with it).

Files (<case>-<theme>-before / -after / -compare)
  toolbar      the Play button in the timeline (tip over the canvas)
  panel        a layer's lock icon in the layers list
  menu         the Undo row of the Edit menu (beside the menu, no arrow, by design)
  edge-left    the pencil in the tool rail (beside it, arrow on its left)
  edge-right   the Find a command button, top right corner (tip held on screen, arrow slid to the right)
  edge-top     the Pixel workspace button at the top (no room above, so below, arrow pointing up)
  edge-bottom  a button 6 px from the bottom edge (a stand-in added for the shot; the page has nothing there)
  slide-<theme>-before.mp4 / -after.mp4   a button glides to the right edge and back with its tip up. Before: the tip stays where it
                                          was. After: the tip is held on screen and the arrow slides along it. H.264, 540x240.

The two judgement calls: the inverted label colours (bold; the dark theme's cream label is the brightest thing on screen while it
shows), and the 12 px gap with an 11 px arrow. Both are a few lines of CSS (.ui-tip in src/ui/ui.css) and TIP_GAP in src/ui/tipPlace.ts.
