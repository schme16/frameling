# Leaning changes size

The same five brush strokes, drawn by a pen held at 0, 30, 50, 70 and 85 degrees of lean, with
**Leaning changes size** on and pressure off. Brush size 16, no taper, no thinning.

- **before**: leaning nudged the stroke's pressure value, so the five lines came out 2, 8, 10, 10 and 10 screen pixels:
  almost no change, and the upright one went thinner than the size that was set.
- **after**: leaning multiplies the mark's size, as it already did on a pixel layer: 8, 10, 12, 14 and 16 pixels, so a
  flat pen draws double.

Dark is Midnight sketchbook, light is Paper and honey.
