Pixel brush, first slice: the dab engine, spacing, tips and the library. Every picture is in both themes (-dark, -light).

spacing      The same round brush at 10%, 25%, 50%, 100% and 200% spacing, top to bottom. Spacing is how far apart the dabs go, as a share of the size.
scatter      Scatter 0, 50%, 100% and 200% (top to bottom); the last row also has size jitter.
picture      Picture tips: stars, hearts, a diamond chain that turns with the line, and a big star scaled up. Each is a little picture stamped along the stroke.
antialias    The same brush with anti-aliasing OFF (top two strokes, sharp pixels) and ON (bottom two, smooth see-through edge), on a 48 pixel sprite so the pixels show.
library      The pixel side of the brush picker, open: the same picker as the vector brushes, listing pixel brushes (starters in groups, soft paint, dry media). The live stroke is the brush picked.

Made with scripts/docs/ab-pixel-brush (see docs/testing.md). The help picture is public/help/pixel-brush-dark.png and -light.png.
Nothing is posted to the board.

Second slice: dynamics, grain, dual tip, colour and wet paint. Both themes again (-dark, -light).
kinds        One stroke of each kind, top to bottom: round, pressure pencil, tapered ink, fast is thin, chalk grain, spatter, bristles, rainbow trail, wet smear.
dynamics     Round with no dynamics, then size from pen pressure, a fade-out taper, speed (fast is thin), and a pressure spray.
texture      Round, chalk grain, pencil on paper (grain and pressure), sponge (a new patch of paper each dab), spatter and bristles (dual tip).
colour       Round, rainbow trail (hue along a fade), autumn leaves (colour wobble), fade to the fill colour, wet smear over blue bars.
feel-clip    The same strokes being drawn, as H.264 clips (-dark.mp4, -light.mp4, with a -poster.png): the point is how the size follows the pen and the speed.
