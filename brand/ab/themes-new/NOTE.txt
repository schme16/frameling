Built: camo and lilac themes use their own chosen colour (aqua #22c3d6, lavender #c9b8e8 / #bba6ea light) with dark
text, instead of gold. Strawberry keeps the gold rule with a sunnier seed yellow (#ffd23f), cels strawberry red,
playhead leafy green. Surround patterns (static CSS gradients, no animation): faint angular camo shards for the camo
pair, little yellow seeds for the strawberry pair, nothing for lavender.
Alternatives considered, not rendered: camo A (built) = charcoal panels with aqua chosen, B = closer to the photo with
deep-teal panels (#0e8fa3 family), rejected as it makes text and cels fight the background. Lavender A (built) =
lavender chosen, B = gold chosen with lavender only as accent (safer, but loses the headset feel). Strawberry: the
seed surround can be switched off by dropping the two ids from SURROUNDS in builtIn.ts if it reads as too busy.
