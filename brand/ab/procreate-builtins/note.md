# Sketch and ink: nine new built-in pixel brushes

`sketch-and-ink.png`: one stroke from each brush, top to bottom: Inking pen, Dry ink, Ink bleed, Colouring pen, Soft 6B pencil,
Light sketch pencil, Felt marker, Dry bristle brush, Canvas brush. The press rises and falls along each stroke.

Modelled on the brushes in the Procreate packs the user collected (the Shenbot set, ChaoPencil, YumyumHime, Lofi, the
shenbotdraws pencil and background brushes), tuned by laying each stroke beside Procreate's own thumbnail of the brush it
follows. The side by side with the thumbnails is on the board only: most of those packs carry no licence, so their
pictures stay out of the repo. Every setting and both picture tips (the bristles and the felt) are Frameling's own.

Left out: the Albaharu texture pack (photo patterns, not a kind of brush) and Catherine's basics, which the importer
already brings in as they are (CC0).
