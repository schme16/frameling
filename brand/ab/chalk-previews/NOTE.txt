Chalk and charcoal previews in the brush Library (board card chalk-previews-stale). Branch wip/chalk-previews.

Cause: not baked pictures. The Library drew its stroke and round thumbnail with drawShape(), which paints the bare line and skips the
shape's effects. Chalk and charcoal now get their grain from a Paper tooth effect on the line, so the picker showed the older
tiny-shape grain (the blobby worm with white specks). Fix: every brush picture goes through one helper, drawPreviewStroke() in
src/brushes/thumbs.ts, which draws through drawShapeFx() like the canvas does, so it can not drift again. The brush options
preview (Tool options) had the same bug and uses the same helper.

after-{dark,light}.png   FIXED. Left and middle: the Library's "Chalk and charcoal" folder (all 7 brushes, shot twice because the panel
                         shows about five rows), stroke preview and round thumbnail. Right: the same 7 brushes drawing a real stroke
                         on the canvas (pretend pen, same swell), top to bottom Chalk, Charcoal, Soft pastel, Oil pastel, Crayon,
                         Vine charcoal, Compressed charcoal. The preview is the brush scaled to fit its row (about 0.6 of its size),
                         so its line is thinner than the real one; the grain, edge and tone match.
before-{dark,light}.png  BEFORE the fix, the same layout: previews are fat blobs with white holes, the canvas strokes are grainy chalk.
