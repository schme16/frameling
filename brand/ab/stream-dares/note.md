Dares, one clip per family, dark and light, shot under xvfb with scripts/docs/record.cjs (help entries in scripts/docs/help/dares.cjs), H.264, captioned.
A (a-view-dares-*): the dares that only change the screen, which work for everyone. A challenge banner, a countdown, then the view flipped, greyscale and zoomed in, each put back by "Put every dare back". Afterwards the page is exactly as it was.
B (b-drawing-dares-*): the dares that reach the drawing. First the audience sends googly eyes with "Let the audience interrupt your drawing" off: turned away (the On air card counts it as not available). Then the switch goes on and the same dare gets through, followed by a splat, a shape, a jelly wobble and a budge, each on its own. Last, five Undos take them all back, one each.
