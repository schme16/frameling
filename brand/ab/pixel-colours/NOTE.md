# Pixel colours: Stroke and Fill today, or Paint and Back

- **A**: the real Palette panel. A swatch click sets the Stroke; the bucket pours the separate Fill, so "I picked green and the bucket made yellow" (see brand/pixel-review/shots/04-bucket-used-yellow-not-green.png). The only hint is a tiny "S" badge on a swatch.
- **B** (mock): two big wells at the top of the Palette, Paint in front and Back behind, with the three habits spelled out: X swaps, right-click paints Back, hold Alt to pick a colour. In the pixel workspace every tool paints with Paint; no tool silently switches wells.
