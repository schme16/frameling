# Pixel UI per way: what the canvas, timeline and boards show

Real Pixel workspace screens (Tiny sprite, Game hero sprite set, Blob sprites demo) with a mock chip or panel injected.

- **A**: a mode chip at the start of the Pixel bar ("One picture", "Animation, 23 frames", "Frames on boards", "Sprites on a sheet"). The layout follows the way: One picture folds the timeline away (a real fold); An animation keeps the timeline and Preview; the two board ways put the Artboards panel up front. `A-*.png` is the four screens in a 2x2 grid.
- **B**: a "Sprite" panel on the right (diagram, plain facts, Add frame/board/sprite, Export, "Change how this sprite is built...", a Quill line). `B-*.png` is the four panels side by side.

The chip and the Sprite panel are mocks (no code behind them); everything else is the real app, so the "Frame 1 of 4" status bar, "128 x 128" artboard sizes and the blank Preview are real findings, not mock errors.
