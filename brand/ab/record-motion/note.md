# Record a move: raw or tidied

Board task `fn-record-motion`. Same drag, same ball, both clips show the recording, the keys on the timeline and the playback. Each exists dark and light.

- **A, raw (`a-raw-*.mp4`).** Detail at its top: a key on every frame, 39 of them for a 3 second swoop. It follows the hand exactly, jitters and all, but it is a wall of keys that is slow to edit and hard to change the timing of.
- **B, tidied (`b-tidied-*.mp4`, the default).** Detail at half: about 10 keys, one for each turn and change of speed, with in-betweens and easing between them. Plays almost the same and is easy to edit. The Detail slider on the bar slides between the two.
