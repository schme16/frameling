# Pixel chooser: how a new pixel file starts

Mock-ups drawn on top of the real File > New sheet (real app CSS, dark and light). Files: `A-*.png`, `B-*.png`, `ab-*.png`.

- **A (recommended)**: a "Pixel art" card on the New sheet opens Quill's question, "Pixel time! What are we making?", with four answers each showing a tiny diagram: One picture, An animation (tagged "Like Aseprite"), Frames on boards, Sprites on a sheet. Then a size row. One extra click, but each way is explained before you commit.
- **B**: no extra step. The New sheet gets a "Pixel art" group of four starter cards (same four ways, with sizes) next to Doodle and Square. Faster for people who already know what they want.
