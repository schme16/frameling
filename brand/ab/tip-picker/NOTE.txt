Tip picker: the two rows of tip chips are one picker button.

BEFORE: a row of nine chips (Plain, Chalk, Charcoal, Watercolour, Stamp, Pen nib, Along the line, Edged, Bristles),
and on a stamp a second row of seven shape chips. Words only, so nothing shows what a line will look like.
AFTER: one button, the same shape as the brush picker above it, showing a stroke drawn in the tip in use. It opens a
list with a real stroke across every tip (drawn by the same renderer as a line on the page, with your size and
pressure), in four groups, with search. A stamp is its shape (Leaf, Dash, Dot, Star, Diamond, Heart, Paw print, and
Yours when you made one), so the shape row is gone. The sliders and the explaining line under them are as before.

Space, the whole Tip block (label, picker or chips, sliders, explaining line), same window, same width, in px:
  stamp tip:            794 -> 698  (96 back)
  chalk or any other:   359 -> 323  (36 back)
  plain:                142 -> 106  (36 back)
Measured by a log step in scripts/docs/ab-tip-picker/shots.cjs (the Tip block's height).

Cost: choosing a tip is two clicks (open the picker, click a row) where a chip was one. Ctrl+K "Brush tip: Chalk", and
Next and Previous brush tip (Alt+Shift+Down and Up) are still one step. In the picker you can also type and press Enter.

Files (each in dark and light):

  picker-open-*.png        picker open: one column (default row style) and two columns (the existing row style choice)
  stamp-chosen-*.png       chalk in use, then Heart picked from the picker
Made with: scripts/docs/ab-tip-picker/shots.cjs (see docs/testing.md).
