Strawberry seeds and the Textures switch, shot in the built app (scripts/shoot.cjs), cropped to the area round the page.

- `light-before` / `light-after`: Strawberry (light). Before, the yellow seeds all but vanish on the cream; after, they
  keep more of their yellow and get a hair-thin outline.
- `dark-before` / `dark-after`: Strawberry Night. The seeds drop back, so they stay a texture rather than sit forward
  of the page.
- `textures-off`: the same light theme with **Preferences > Textures** off.
