# Pen and tablet tidy

Shot in the built app under xvfb, Preferences > Pen and tablet, cropped to the settings pane. Both start from settings
saved by the old version (every tool's stabilizer saved as 0, a curve dragged out of shape), which is what the user had.

- `pen-table-dark.png`, `pen-table-light.png`: A (left) is the table as it was, every stabilizer at Off. B (right) is the
  same saved settings now, a gentle 12% on every tool, with the words above the table saying what the stabilizer does.
- `pen-curve-dark.png`, `pen-curve-light.png`: A (left) is the dragged-out curve with nothing to put it back. B (middle) has
  **Reset** under the graph and a plainer Feel hint. C (right) is after pressing Reset: the straight line is back, **Normal**
  is lit, **Reset** waits (greyed) because the curve is already plain, and the lightest press, full press and leaning
  sliders are exactly where they were.
