# Bone tools: the tool area, before and after

- `before-dark.png`, `before-light.png`: the rail's Rig group has one Bone button, and the options hide the rest behind **Draw bones / Adjust bones** (Alt for a moment) and the Delete key.
- `after-dark.png`, `after-light.png`: Add bone, Edit bone and Remove bone are three buttons in the rail (and a switch at the top of the options), with **Add next bone** and **Remove bone** one click away.
