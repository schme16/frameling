# The Themes page

- **current-card**: the two themes you are using, as one card cut corner to corner by a squiggle: your light pick on
  the top left, your dark pick on the bottom right, each showing its page, a panel and its brightest colours.
- **advanced**: **Picking** is new. Basic lists the themes made for each mode, as before. Advanced lists every theme
  under both, so a bright theme can be your night one if that is what you want.
- The page's preamble is a tooltip on the heading now, and the copy button says **Make it mine**. The buttons on a
  theme card wait below it and swoop up when you point at the card, tab into it, or pick it.
- **after-card-dark / after-card-light**: the card as you asked for it. The squiggle is a gentle wave instead of a
  jagged zigzag, and each half is the real app preview drawn in the theme you picked, so the card shows your actual
  two themes rather than colour blocks.
- **line-card-dark / line-card-light**: the join is drawn as a black ink line, 3 px, over both halves, so the
  squiggle reads as one pen stroke rather than just where two pictures meet.
- **wavy-card-dark / wavy-card-light / wavy-zoom-dark**: three bends instead of two, and each half is now clipped to
  its own side of the wave, so the line really is where one theme stops and the other starts. The zoom shows the cut
  close up: the paper is white in both themes, so it reads as continuous where the wave crosses the drawing.
- **paper-card-dark / paper-card-light / paper-zoom-dark**: the paper in this card takes a little of each theme's own
  colour, so the cut along the wave can be seen even across the drawing, where both themes would otherwise draw the
  same white page. The gallery cards keep their true white paper.
- **fixed-card-dark / fixed-card-light / fixed-zoom-dark**: the real cause of the spill, found by reading the boxes
  back out of the running app: each half was 190 x 124 inside a 190 x 119 art box, so its clip stretched the wave
  over a box 5 px taller than the one the inked line was drawn in, and the two drifted apart in the middle. The
  halves are now pinned to the art box, so the cut and the line are the same curve.
