Gouache used to draw the same flat stroke as Marker and Block marker, so it read as paint in general and not as gouache.
It now has a character of its own: a solid body with only a trace of brush marks, finished matte (a little paler and
chalkier than it went on, with a faint velvety grain). The colour is still fully solid, so it covers what is underneath.
