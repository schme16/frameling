# Zooming a heavy drawing: before and after

Same gesture on the same file (the user's undo-test, 26 chalk and charcoal lines with paper tooth): twenty-two wheel
notches in, a hold, twenty-two out, filmed for ten seconds off a virtual screen.

before.mp4: development. Every zoom step redraws the paper tooth of every line, so the zoom crawls (a frame is a quarter to
three quarters of a second) and has not finished zooming out when the ten seconds are up.

after.mp4: this branch. The zoom keeps up with the wheel, the picture is soft while it moves and sharp a moment after it stops.
