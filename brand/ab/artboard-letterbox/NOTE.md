# Shots of different sizes: A/B

`ab.png`: the same joined film (320 x 180 picture, three shots: wide 320 x 180, square 180 x 180, tall 120 x 180), one frame of each shot.
A (built, the default): Fit (bars), the whole shot shows with bars in the first shot's background colour. B: Fill (crop), the shot covers the picture and the overhang is cropped.
