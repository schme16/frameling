Strawberry seeds after the user's note on look-seeds-textures ("more contrast on the light, less yellow and more white
on the dark"). Shot in the built app (scripts/shoot.cjs), cropped to the area round the page.

- `light-before` / `light-after`: Strawberry (light). The seed yellow is deepened with a touch of the berry accent and
  laid on nearly solid, so the seeds read clearly on the cream.
- `dark-before` / `dark-after`: Strawberry Night. The seed colour is mixed mostly from the cream text colour now, so
  the seeds come out as pale flecks instead of dark brown.
