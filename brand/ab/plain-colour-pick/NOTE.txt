Selecting plain-colour shapes: the Colours panel and the fill and stroke boxes show what the shapes have.

A (before-pick-*): one red shape picked with the Select tool while the colour box holds a blue. The fill box, the
   picker and the hex field all show the blue, not the red shape's colour.
B (after-pick-*): the same pick. The fill box, the picker and the hex field show the shape's red (#e0382f). The box
   itself is untouched until you change a colour.
before-differ-*: three shapes (red, green, purple) all picked. The panel shows the box's blue and one change would overwrite all three.
after-differ-*: the same pick. The fill box is striped ("mixed"), the picker steps aside, and the panel says
   "These 3 shapes have different colours" with Make them all match the first. Clicking a swatch gives them all that colour.

Both themes (dark = Midnight Sketchbook, light = Paper and Honey), cropped to the canvas and the Colours panel.
